# Refund Requests on a team licence freeze its seats, not the buyer's access

A team-licence purchase issues seats (coupons). It never gave the buyer the course, so a Refund Request on it doesn't take the buyer's own access away. The hold calculation ignores any purchase that has coupons. Instead, while a team-licence purchase has an Open or Approved Refund Request, its unclaimed seats can't be claimed, and the redeem page explains why. A decline makes them claimable again. Seats that were already claimed are left alone. We chose this over revoking access for everyone on the team, because those learners didn't raise the request.

## Consequences

- If the money for a team licence comes back, the seats people already claimed are still used. If that's not acceptable, the decision needs revisiting.
